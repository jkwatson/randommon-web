dungeonRumor
  Nobody who has gone in during the last year has come back. The most recent group were professionals.
  There is a buyer in town who pays well for anything brought out — he won't say what specifically he's looking for.
  A child claims to have seen lights moving inside at night. Adults dismiss it. She has been right before.
  One of the groups occupying it has been quietly asking around for people willing to work. They won't say what the work involves.
  Someone came out a few weeks ago, would not speak of what they'd seen, and left town before morning.
  There's supposed to be a hoard inside. Every group that has gone for it has turned on each other before reaching it.
  The entrance has been sealed and reopened three times in living memory. Nobody agrees on why.
  Locals avoid the area at night — something about sounds that carry. No one will describe them specifically.
  A sage in town will pay handsomely for a particular object reportedly inside. He's been trying to commission the retrieval for years without success.
  A priest passing through called the place cursed, refused to perform a warding, and left in a hurry.
  Someone who went in two months ago has been sending letters back — cheerful, vague, and increasingly unlike themselves.
  The faction that used to control it collapsed suddenly. Nobody knows what happened to them or what they left behind.
  A rival group went in recently. They had a map. It was wrong.
  There's a prisoner in there someone wants recovered. The reward is real; the explanation for who they are and why they're inside is not forthcoming.
  The last group to come out said they found something that scared them out of the profession entirely. They now farm. They won't elaborate.
  Locals say the place has a caretaker of some kind — not hostile exactly, but particular about conduct. People who show respect get out.
  An old man in the tavern claims to have been inside decades ago. His account is detailed, internally consistent, and increasingly improbable.
