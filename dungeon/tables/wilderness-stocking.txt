  expand their range into neighboring land; they are running out of space
  eliminate the rival factions one by one until this territory is entirely theirs
  protect a nesting site, hoard, or sacred place that outsiders must never reach
  serve the power that dwells at the heart of this land — they are its congregation
  survive; their numbers are dwindling and they are growing desperate
  drive out or subjugate every other faction and claim sole dominion over the region
  keep outsiders away from a specific place deep in the territory — they know what's there
  hoard the food, water, or shelter that everything else here depends on
  breed; they need more space, more food, more territory to sustain their growth

  destroy whatever is bound or hidden at the region's most remote point
  locate a specific document, map, or relic hidden somewhere within the territory
  capture a specific creature or person for use as a weapon or bargaining chip
  reach a specific landmark first and claim whatever's there before a rival does
  prevent a rival faction from completing whatever they came here to do
  ensure nothing native to this land ever leaves it — regardless of the cost
  make contact with the entity said to dwell at the heart of this territory
  map this territory completely before the rival expedition does

wildernessHookGeneric
  A trapper who works that area hasn't been seen in two weeks. His family is asking quietly, not officially.
  Something has been driving game down out of there — the animals look frightened, not hunted.
  A merchant's wagon went into the region a fortnight ago and hasn't emerged. The merchant had a contract with someone in town.
  There is reportedly a structure somewhere in there that predates the current settlement by centuries.

  Strange lights have been seen moving through the area at night. Locals disagree on whether they're getting closer.
  Someone has been leaving supplies at the edge of the region — food, firewood, occasionally a tool. No one knows for whom.
  A map of the region is for sale at the cartographer's. It's detailed and shows a large structure that no confirmed expedition has ever found.
  Travelers who pass through report arriving at their destination having lost more time than the journey should have taken.
  A faction is offering good coin to anyone who can scout a specific part of that region. They won't describe what they're looking for.
  A former resident of a settlement that used to exist in that region is trying to hire people to help retrieve something they left behind.
  Hunters report that the wildlife in there is behaving strangely — not fleeing people, just watching.
  There's a standing bounty posted at the garrison. It doesn't specify what for, only that the collector will know it when they see it.
  A pilgrim heading into the region said she wasn't coming back out. She seemed at peace with this. She was not the first to say it.
